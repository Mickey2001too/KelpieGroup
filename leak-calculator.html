Booking Leak Calculator

A free tool from Michelle for independent salon and day spa owners with one to six chairs or rooms and no full-time receptionist. Enter your own numbers from one week's tally (a notebook by the phone is enough). The calculator counts where bookings slipped away, ranks your three leaks, and writes a first plan for your biggest one. Your answers never leave this device.

1. What is one booked appointment worth on average?

Your average service price, in dollars. Use your own average — no industry figure is assumed here.

2. How many calls went to voicemail or unanswered last week?

From your phone log: every call you could not pick up while you were with a client.

3. Of those unanswered calls, how many did you win back and book?

Callers who came back and booked after all. The rest are your missed-call leak.

4. How many no-shows or late cancellations left a chair empty last week?

Appointments that sat empty because the client did not show or cancelled too late to refill.

5. How many clients left last week without a next appointment booked?

The rebooking question, asked before the client leaves. Everyone who left without a next booking counts here.
