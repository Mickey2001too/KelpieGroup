Questions salon and spa owners ask

The things owners ask before they book a free audit. If yours isn't here, send a message.

I keep missing calls while my hands are busy. What do you actually do about that?

That's the whole job. I build you a booking engine that answers when you can't, takes the request, and gets it into your booking system, built on tools I own and you control — and nothing runs without your say-so. Missed calls become booked appointments, and you can count them in your own system. Start with a free 30-minute Workflow Audit and we'll count your missed calls together.

What does it cost?

It depends on what you need, which is why it starts with a free 30-minute Workflow Audit. After that I quote you a price for the setup. The one fixed price: a 1-on-1 Power Session is $97. No hourly billing, no surprise subscriptions.

Do I have to buy new software?

No. I build on tools I own and you control, and I work with the booking system you already use. If you need something new, I'll tell you plainly and you approve it first.

Will it message my clients or post without me checking?

Never. You approve everything before it goes live. Drafts first, your tap, then it runs.

Thirty minutes, Monday to Friday, 1 to 4 PM Eastern, on video. We look at how your calls and bookings flow right now, I map what AI can take off your plate, and you get the plan in plain English. Book it here: https://calendar.app.google/PJZyF1Ht2vgJFSdE9

I'm not techy. Do I have to learn a system?

No. I'm a retired teacher — plain English, step by step, and a walkthrough so you can run it yourself. You own what we build.

Michelle Butts, Kelpie Group. Retired special education and technology teacher. Four years doing this work, including the years I ran a day spa — Kelpie Group, the AI business, started in October 2026. I work online, serving small business owners.
